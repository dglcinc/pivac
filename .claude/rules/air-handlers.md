## nginx proxy

- nginx AirMaster proxy: `/etc/nginx/snippets/airmaster.conf`, included by the `pivac` site, with the cookie map in `/etc/nginx/conf.d/airmaster-map.conf` (both Pi-local, added 2026-11-15; site backup `pivac.bak-20261115-airmaster`). One `location /airmaster/<host>/` block per unit, where `<host>` is the unit's host name because the page's tiles link to `../<host>/`: `auth_basic` on `/etc/nginx/.htpasswd`, `proxy_pass http://<ip>/`, `proxy_set_header Authorization ""`, `proxy_set_header Cookie $airmaster_cookie` (the unit's `s` cookie alone, since the unit refuses any request line over 400 characters), and `proxy_cookie_path / /airmaster/<host>/` so each unit keeps its own login. The icon and manifest paths are served without Basic Auth so a phone's home-screen icon loads. It needs AirMaster firmware 1.2.4 or later. A new or renamed unit needs its block and its name in the two regex lists there, then a reload.

## Unico SCB (`docs/unico-scb-protocol.md`, `docs/unico-scb-live-test.md`)

- **The S.M.A.R.T. Control Board is the bus master.** It sends a 36-byte status frame every 0.85 s whether or not anything answers, carrying the six thermostat inputs (G, Y1, Y2, W1, W2, E), actual airflow in CFM and motor RPM, so a passive listener needs no transmission. The 51-byte frame (switch word, Air Cycle values, DC bus, firmware) comes only after a PC reply and stops about 9 s after the last one; the idle frame requests nothing and brings it. Every PC frame is a write request, and a changed airflow is stored within a second. `scripts/unico-scb.py` (`listen`, `idle`, `mirror`, `set`, `backup`, `restore`, `override`) speaks it from the M2 on `/dev/cu.usbmodemunicousb1`, a Cypress CDC board macOS drives without an installer, and `pivac.UnicoSCB` reads it on the Pi from `/dev/serial/by-id/*unicousb*` (one config section per air handler, `port:` for USB or `node:` for a rev B node, same paths). Writes of every setting the protocol has sit behind `--transmit`; override, the undocumented test bytes and a bulk restore also need a typed confirmation word. The SCB's heat and cool relays are the zone's W and Y inputs at the HZ-432, so `override` is a real zone call and sits behind two flags, a 300 s cap and an explicit release. Proven 2026-10-24 on the utility zone's M2430, firmware 3.21: on its cool call the frame showed `G,Y1` and the airflow rose to the stored 300 CFM in 20 s.

| `LoopDelta` | **Derived** — reads Signal K rather than hardware, so it never contends for the 1-wire bus or the GPIO pins and opens no second Honeywell session. Publishes `environment.inside.hvac.{primary,LOOPA,LOOPB}.deltaT` every cycle, taking **three values with three meanings: a number is a live measurement, `0` means the loop is not measuring** (idle, or within `settle_s` of a start while the probes still hold their stagnant values), **and a GAP means a source is stale or the module is down**. Publishing 0 for idle rather than nothing is what keeps that third case readable, since idle-as-gap conflates a stopped pump with a dead service. In **Kelvin**: a difference, so °F is `*9/5` with **no** offset. `.flowing` (0/1) is emitted every cycle. Gated on the `HPCALL` relay (the primary also on `DHWX`; `relay` takes a list), narrowed to each secondary's `zones` (any call) and `heat_zones` (heat call only: zones that cool on a Bosch). **Also publishes the master bedroom air-handler node's two coil pairs** as `environment.inside.hvac.ah.mbr.{water,air}.deltaT` and `.flowing`, return minus supply, gated on `HPCALL` narrowed to `MASTER_BR`; a probe name in `loops:` is dotted where its path is nested. **The utility node's pairs gate on the SCB's own call bits through a `gate:` list** (`ah.utility.call.y1`/`call.w1` for the water, `call.g` for the air), which replaces `relay:` and the zone narrowing for that loop and shuts when the reader is dead.
