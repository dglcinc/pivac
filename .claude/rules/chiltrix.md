# Chiltrix CX75 and the hydronic plant

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-NC-4.0 -->
<!-- Copyright David Lewis -->

Loaded when a file under its `paths:` is read; `CLAUDE.md` says to read it first for any work on the chiller, the glycol loop, loop pressure or the heating and cooling plant. Rules here; the measurements and incident history behind them are in the docs named.

## Rules

- **Modbus link:** `A3`/`B3` on connector `P5`, A/B only, no ground, no bias, no terminator (the chiller biases the pair itself at 3.18 V). Modbus RTU 9600 8N1, slave 1, **function 03 only, never 6 or 16**, though 140–146 are writable. Ground the cable shield **at the Arduino end only**: floating it lost 65 % of frames at 55 Hz and none at idle, so link quality measured at idle is meaningless. `SW1` on the main board is the compressor model encode; there is no Modbus parameter on the panel. Diagnose with the failure code (`TMO` vs `CRC`), and use Chiltrix's own register document, never the community maps.
- **Register map:** 140 on/off, 141 mode, 142 cooling target (whole °C), 202 ambient, 205 outlet, 281 inlet (all ÷10 °C), 213 flow (÷10 L/min), 227 compressor Hz, 256 current (÷10 A). `inlet` is water going TO the chiller and `outlet` water returning FROM it, so `281 − 205` is positive in cooling; compare temperatures at idle, never during a transient. Every address 0–359 answers, so a response proves nothing and `registerCount` is link health. 184 addresses are published (the confirmed set, `P00`–`P139` as `raw.r<addr>`, and 215–217 as the `C16` defrost and `C17` freeze-protection candidates); address = parameter number is verified, but trust numeric matches over raw enum values. Register `65` reads 14 against the manual's `P65` = 20; confirm on the panel before reasoning about the low-flow trip.
- **Return-water target 12 °C / 53.6 °F** (since 2026-09-14; `P12` widens to 3 next). The controller stores whole °C: set it in °C and read it back. `P59` antifreeze trips at 3 °C on leaving water, which runs ~9 °F below the return target, so 49 °F is the floor; raise the target before widening `P12`. E14 needs a breaker power cycle; the panel's Error reset does not clear it. To run colder: glycol → `P59` → target. Glycol alone does not help. The loop was last drained and refilled 2026-10-10: 25 % PG, about 75 gal. A drain follows `docs/hydronic-drain-and-refill.md`; any glycol change moves the `startupFlow` baseline, so record its date. Full text: `docs/chiltrix-modbus.md`, Operating rules in full.
- **`P5` is the low-flow alarm (`P65` trip, 20 L/min) and the Y-strainer is the first suspect.** Read `C13` flow in the 1–2 min pump-only window at the start of a run (54 L/min after cleaning); it reads 0 at idle. **The loop is filled to 20 psi cold on an expansion tank precharged to about 18 psi (since 2026-10-12); keep the cold pressure 1.5–2 psi above the precharge. The autofill regulator must never be set at or above the cold fill and must never be raised; top up manually with premixed glycol.** For any fill larger than a top-up, remove the Caleffi demineralisation filter and fill through the bypass valve behind the autofill's pressure regulator. `docs/hydronic-pressure-reset-checklist.md` lowers the pressure without moving fluid. Full text: `docs/chiltrix-modbus.md`, Operating rules in full.
- **The Y-strainer screen must be 60 mesh or finer (0.25 mm opening) per the CX65 and CX50-2 IOMs**, which tie the exchanger warranty to it; the exchanger's own blocking limit is about 1.2 mm (Alfa Laval CB class), so 20 mesh stops a blockage and 60 mesh stops progressive fouling and protects the flow meter. **The ACOL DN32 strainer's stock screen measures 60 mesh** (wire pitch 0.38–0.44 mm, 23–26 wires per cm, opening about 0.24 mm, measured against a millimetre tape on 2026-09-12), so it meets the IOM and needs no replacement on mesh grounds. The basket is 43.44 mm OD, 39 mm ID and 72.14 mm long, a stainless wire cloth backed by expanded metal, if one is ever ordered. **Fouling has slowed to nearly nothing**: 21 days after the 2026-08-22 cleaning the screen was about 5 % covered, against a full blockage in the first 18 days, so the loop is shedding its initial load and admitting no new scale; check again in a month. A finer screen fills faster, so pair it with a dirt separator upstream; `.startupFlow` is the sentinel either way.
- **Flow while running is a controlled output**, so raw flow, flow ÷ Hz, inlet against target, end-of-cycle inlet and run duration all fail as fouling signals. The two sentinels are `.startupFlow` (clean 52–55 L/min, under 40 L/min is the alarm; it is blind to a total loss of flow, which `chiltrix-zero-flow` covers; 6.9 L/min is the idle baseline under `P52` = 2) and the hydronic pressure step at pump start (baseline 1.0–1.6 psi, valid only for starts from 19.5 psi or more). **Register `284` is the fault/lockout word**: 2 is P1 high pressure, 16 is P4 over-current, 32 was the closed-valve lockout; `.faultCode` holds it for 60 s. Two P1s or two P4s within about 30 minutes latch until a breaker power cycle, and `chiltrix-fault-latched` emails when `284` holds non-zero for six minutes. During a lockout the pump runs at its `P53` minimum, about 30 L/min, which is not a fouled strainer. Heating target 46 °C since 2026-10-29 (entered in °C); `P38` 15 °C, `P39` 12 °C, `P53` 50 %, `P52` 2. **Proposed and not made, pending Chiltrix's review: `P53` 50 → 80, `P96` 54 → 90, `P27` 100 → 90.** To give Chiltrix a lockout, export the raw registers with `scripts/chiltrix-raw-fetch.sh` (on the Pi) and `scripts/chiltrix-raw-build.py`. Mechanisms of P1 and P4: `docs/chiltrix-heating-control-analysis.md`. Full text: `docs/chiltrix-modbus.md`, Operating rules in full.
- **Winter: the chiller stays on in heat mode all winter; the HZ-432 balance point is 40 °F (since 2026-11-25, on trial for cutover behaviour and energy use; the plan's figure is 35 °F); the five hydronic thermostats sit on Heat, never Auto; loop B's UP26-99F runs on HIGH in heating and LOW for cooling** (drop to MEDIUM if loop B's supply runs 2 °F or more under `IN` with both loops calling). Judge the balance point by the rooms and the tank, never duty: 35 °F is too low if a room falls 1 °F below setpoint overnight or the upper tank stays under 105 °F outside a defrost. A latched lockout above the balance point stops all heat. Taking the unit out of service is HMI off with the breaker on, `DHWX` inhibit open, HZ-432 boiler-only. Left enabled in cool mode in the cold it courts E14, which `chiltrix-cooling-cold` watches. A heat↔cool changeover costs 25–45 min of compressor time, and changeovers decide the saving. A sub-minute `operatingMode` = 1 blip with `HPHEAT` energised is a mains interruption; check `journalctl --list-boots`. Measured COP, cost and the record: `docs/chiltrix-shoulder-season-heating-plan.md` §7–9 and `docs/chiltrix-modbus.md`, Operating rules in full.
- **Serial bridge:** UNO R4 on the Pi's USB at `/dev/ttyACM0` (serial `E8F60AA93AA8`); a charge-only cable is the first failure to check. Sketches in `~/github/Arduino` (`ChiltrixModbus`, `ChiltrixScan`, `RS485Blast`). Opening the port does not reset the board, and a running `watch` eats one character, so any serial client must quiesce to the prompt first and time out its reads. Drop `TMO` rows before analysing a log. `chiltrix-logger.service` is retired; `pivac-chiltrix` declares `Conflicts=` on it.

## Modules

| Module | Source |
|--------|--------|
| `ChiltrixModbus` | Chiltrix CX75 over RS-485 Modbus RTU (A3/B3, 9600 8N1, slave 1, function 03), bridged by an UNO R4 on USB running the `ChiltrixScan` sketch. **Read only — function 03, never 6 or 16**, though 140–146 are writable. **Block mode since 2026-11-06**: drives the sketch's `r <lo> <n>` command, one function 03 request per block, and sweeps all 234 registers of 0–146 and 200–286 in five blocks of up to 50 about once a second (`daemon_sleep: 0.25`). The confirmed set publishes under named paths (`hvac.chiller.chiltrix.*`, temperatures in **Kelvin**) and every other address under `.raw.r<addr>`. Also emits `.evaporatorDelta`, `.runDuration`, `.startupFlow` and `.faultCode`. **A register goes to Signal K only when its value changes, and every value is resent once `refresh_s` (30 s) has passed**, so InfluxDB holds an exact 1 s record when forward-filled, without a point per register per second; `registerCount` (234 on a clean sweep) is sent every sweep and marks each one, and `.faultCode` holds a non-zero 284 for `fault_hold_s` (60 s). `mode: scan` restores the one-register-per-transaction `s <from> <to>` scan (about 60 ms per register). |
