# Procedure to drain and refill hydronic system
1. Prep the setup
	* move the workbench for easy access to system and move the toolbox and compressor out of the way
	* position the 45 gal trash can next to the system and put two pails rich glycol and 5 pails purified water (21.43%)
	* leave the seven buckets near the system for filling
	* position the pump, throttle valve, and wireless on/off near the system with in and out hoses. Hoses long enough to have the intake in the trash can with pump on floor (six foot hose?) and output connected to system input (
	* have an extra hose handy and connect it to the drain valve on the hydro tank
	* Position ladder under MBR hatch
	* have tools ready to open coil section and open coil vent
	* stage the compressor with hose, and jegs with regulator or california portable to blow air through the coil vents in the attic.

2. Shut everything down
	* turn off boiler switch
	* turn off all air handlers using TCC
	* power down chiller from breaker panel
	* close the valves on the water makeup
3. Drain system
	* use the extra hose at the base of the hydro tank to fill the first 5 gal bucket and then close the valve. After initial pressure is released, open vent at top of hydro tank
	* open all five zone valves; a closed zone valve traps its coil full
	* climb each of the attic hatches and open the coil vent for each of the three attic air handlers
	* continue draining from tank drain using pump. May need to move the pump to the valve drain next to the tank if it starts cavitating. KEEP TRACK OF PAIL COUNT (write it down).
	* Once initial drain is done, compute how much is left.
	* Shift drain to true lowest point to catch remaining fluid, close tank inputs and outputs to avoid tank refilling.
	* Close vents and valves and push air at 20 psi from compressor to a drain port as needed to extract remaining fluid (not using pump). Blow from the vents on the air handlers toward each loop's low drain, in the direction of normal flow: the Taco's integral check valve blocks air pushed the other way. Use a reg on the Jegs, or I can use the portable one in the garage.
	* Open wye cleanout on compressor (capture fluid and add to a pail). close tank valves to chiller and use air at overhead fill valves to push any remaining fluid out. Review manual to see if drain plugs should be opened too or if air is sufficient (probably governed by how much total fluid I extract from the system.)
	* Check and set expansion tank pressure to 21 psi, 1–2 psi below the 22–23 psi cold fill, so the bladder holds some fluid when the loop is cold.
	* Do a 30 minute Rydlime flush on the boiler loop only, with the chiller and tank isolated, and rinse with water until it runs neutral so no acid meets the new glycol. Service air separators. Install union if practical to main loop air separator to facilitate future service.
4. Refill system
	* replace  wye filter. open the chiller side valves on the tank and use the throttled pump to fill the tank and chiller loop till fluid comes out the hydro tank air vent.
	* Close tank and hydro air vent, chiller loop is done.
	* Use drain on system-side auto-fill section to fill rest of system. do the kids and MBR loop first.
	* Close all header valves except the loop being filled supply. Connect return hose to valve on loop return for that loop, other end to trash can.
	* Use throttled pump to fill loop, close vents as system fills. Once vents are closed, keep pump going until fluid returns without bubbles.
	* Do other loop.
	* Close remaining supply valve and verify main loop gets filled.
	* Watch the boiler gauge while the pump runs on this step: the dead-headed pump makes about 52 psi and the boiler relief opens at 30 psi, so keep the throttle under 30. Bring the gauge to 22–23 psi cold, close the throttle valve, then stop the pump.
	* Calculate difference between fluid removed and fluid added, check all possible air gaps if the difference is significant.

5. Re-commission system
	* Make sure all system valves are in normal operating position. Double check. Leave zone valves open for now.
	* Open auto-fill supply valve and wait for vents to stop venting.
	* Power on chiller to allow it to start charging hydro tank.
	* While that is happening, do one last vent purge at each air handler and then close zone valve and close up air box.
	* Power on system. for any zone not calling, force a call and verify that the coil is not air locked.
