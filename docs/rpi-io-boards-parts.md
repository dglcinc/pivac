new 5-way plugs. Digi-Key pages listed here were found by search on 2026-09-12 and not
re-checked for stock; the Amazon entries are searches, not verified listings.

## Notes

- The PTSM horizontal headers carry the `R32` suffix at distributors, which means a 500-piece
  reel; Digi-Key and Mouser cut tape from it, so order the quantity wanted.
- The PTSM plugs take 24–20 AWG; the link cable is five 22 AWG wires, about 60 mm.
- The Pi socket 2202992 is the only part specific to the housing: its height sets the Pi's
  16 mm stack, so an ordinary 2 × 20 stacking socket will not do.
